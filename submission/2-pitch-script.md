# Kering: 2–3 minute pitch (handbook order: Problem, Solution and demo, AI and progress, Next step)

The pitch runs from the organisers' master deck on the event computer, not your laptop. Plan for slides to carry it and the demo to be a screen recording or a second tab if they allow one. Ask the host on arrival.

## 0:00 Problem (35 s)
"South Bali is building fast, and most new villas pump their own groundwater. The official map says the basin is fine: 'Aman', 10.8% of safe yield used. But that count only includes licensed wells. A villa's own well is invisible to it. In Kerobokan Kelod, 14 of 60 sampled wells have already turned salty. A banjar, a villa owner, a family: none of them can see where the pressure is."

Users: banjar councils, villa and homestay operators, households.

## 0:35 Solution and demo (75 s)
"Kering ranks 261 villages by pressure on the aquifer."
1. **Map.** The coast from Legian to Canggu to Seminyak is darkest: 32 villages are in the top band. Click Seminyak: 47 licensed wells per km².
2. **Satellite.** Pecatu and Ungasan: drag the lens back to 2017. 5,866 of 12,136 buildings were first seen between 2015 and 2025, on ground that was open before.
3. **Is it the rain?** "Dry-season rain in Pecatu has not fallen in 30 years (+55 mm a decade). It is the ground that changed."
4. **Rain planner.** A 3-bedroom villa, 220 m² roof: a 50 m³ tank covers about 35% of its water and avoids about IDR 15.9 M of tanker water a year.

## 1:50 AI and progress (40 s)
"Claude does three jobs: an agent with five tools that answers questions on this data and highlights the answer on the map; a banjar brief in Bahasa, English or Chinese; and a grader that reads an Airbnb description for water use. Working today: the map, satellite, planner and data page, on real data. Not validated: the score is pressure on the aquifer, not water level. Our own pre-registered check says PARTIAL, and we say so in the app."

If they allow a live click: Ask Kering, "Which villages near Canggu are most stressed, and what is the rain doing?" and watch the tool calls and the map highlight.

## 2:30 Next step (20 s)
"With a Bali partner we would test the score against one real measurement: one banjar, one villa operator, a conductivity pen and about 20 wells. Today we want to turn one founder's village into a brief they can use on Monday, in Bahasa and Chinese."

## If you only get 2 minutes
Cut the satellite step (2) and read "Next step" as one sentence.

## Questions to expect
- **Remove Claude. What stops working?** The map, satellite and planner keep working. The briefs, the agent and the listing grader stop. The data is the product; Claude makes it usable by a non-expert.
- **Why trust the ranking?** Detector precision on new build is 60% on a strict 30-site check, rain cells are about 25 km, and there is no water measurement in it. That is why it says "pressure, not level", why the check is PARTIAL, and why the next step is a measurement.
- **Who uses it Monday?** A banjar takes the brief to a council meeting. A villa owner gets a tank size and a rupiah figure.
- **Is the official map wrong?** No. It is right for the whole basin on licensed use. The gap is the unlicensed wells in the strip where the pressure is.
- **The one number to remember?** 10.8% safe yield used (official) vs 14 of 60 wells salty in Kerobokan Kelod.
